Privacy Policy

This integration is built based on BigCat ERP, used only to connect with Mercado Libre Open API for order, shipment and product management.

We only collect necessary data authorized by the seller from Mercado Libre, including order information, shipping data and basic account information. All data will only be used for ERP business operation and will not be shared with third parties.

We will store your data securely, and you may revoke authorization at any time in your Mercado Libre account.
